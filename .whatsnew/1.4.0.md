### More room to work on a ticket

The ticket workspace is less cluttered while you write a response, with reply actions easier to reach. The details panel shows the customer's email address with a copy button. Other tickets appear newest first, with their ticket number, date and time. You can copy ticket numbers and links to share with a colleague.

### More help with replies

Write formatted replies and include attachments. Switch between the original conversation and an English translation, and choose the language for your response.

AI reply tools bring together relevant knowledge and similar previous replies. Review the supporting references, give feedback on suggestions, and use the checks before sending to catch unfinished placeholders. Conversation handovers help the next agent pick up the thread.

### Getting started is easier

Open **Guides** for step-by-step tours. The expanded help guide covers reviewing tickets, replying, changing statuses, using placeholders and other everyday tasks. Expanding an FAQ answer now keeps your place on the page.

### Clearer ticket queues

The outstanding queue includes unfinished tickets, including pending and GDPR tickets, regardless of age. Resolved and closed tickets remain available in History. Customer replies reopen pending tickets, and snoozed tickets return when their snooze expires.

### Keep knowledge ready for the team

Knowledge tools now include file and website sources, saved filters, review ownership and bulk publishing. Review queues help identify articles needing attention and gaps raised while working on replies. Insights includes reporting on AI reply feedback and outcomes.

### A reminder before you sign in again

Agent sessions last eight hours. When Respovia is active, a warning appears during the final 30 minutes so you have time to finish your work before signing in again.

### For platform admins: unrouted mail at a glance

Open **Platform · Brands → Unrouted inbox** to review mail that could not be matched to an active brand. The link shows the outstanding ticket count and refreshes when you return to the page or press **Refresh**. Opening the inbox and refreshing it now keeps you on the requested page.
