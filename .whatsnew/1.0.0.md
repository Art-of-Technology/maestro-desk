### A tidier Respovia

- "Conversations" is now called **Tickets** everywhere.
- Your **workspace logo** now replaces the Respovia lettering in the sidebar, scaled cleanly with no distortion.
- New tickets start on "Select a category…" — a category must be actively chosen, so your reporting stays accurate.
- Outgoing emails lost the coloured stripe at the top: your brand logo now owns the email header.

### Small fixes

- The Hide button no longer overlaps section counters or tabs.
- The Email and Call buttons were removed from customer profiles — the contact details themselves are still right there on the profile.
