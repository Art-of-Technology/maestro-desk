### Customer profiles, rebuilt

- **Profile areas are yours to arrange.** Admins can reorder or hide the sections of the customer profile (tickets, notes, activity, custom fields and more) from **Layouts → Profile areas**. Layout settings now save for the whole workspace instead of resetting on reload.
- **KYC is gone from profiles** — the status field, filter and risk flag were removed everywhere.
- **Several emails and mobiles per customer.** A customer can now hold more than one email address and mobile number, with one marked as primary. Searching by any of them finds the right person, and an email sent from any of a customer's addresses lands on their existing profile instead of creating a duplicate. Adding and editing addresses from the profile arrives in the next update.

### Creating tickets

- **New ticket flow rebuilt.** Pick the customer with a live search, then write the message in a second step. Send it straight away or **save it as a draft** — and tickets you create now stay put.
- Start a ticket straight from a customer's profile with **✉ New ticket**.

### Deleting and merging, done properly

- **Deleting is real and safe.** Tickets, customers and notes can now be deleted for good, always behind an "are you sure?" confirmation, and only by roles with the new **Delete & merge** permission.
- A customer with ticket history can't be deleted by accident — merge them into the right profile instead.
- **Merging customers is now a real merge.** Every ticket, note and contact detail moves to the surviving profile, and **Un-merge** puts it all back if it was a mistake.
